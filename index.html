Hunting vs. patching: same models, two jobs

8 models scored on both
  • Top scores are low on every defensive benchmark. The best threat hunter covers 45% of attack activity. The best patcher fixes 48% of vulnerabilities. Neither benchmark has a model above half.
  • Being good at one job doesn't predict the other. Gemini 3.7 Flash is near the top on patching (44.0%) but mid-table on hunting (0.291). Kimi K3 is mid-pack on hunting and near the bottom on patching.
  • GPT‑5.6 Sol is the only one of these eight in the top two on both. Grok 4.6 hunts almost as well but patches less.
  • The offense–defense gap is wide. Claude Opus 4.6 solves 93% of Cybench's offensive CTFs but covers only 0.445 of attack activity on the Cyber Defense Benchmark.
